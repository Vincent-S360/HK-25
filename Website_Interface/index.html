About SALAHE

SALAHE is your intelligent farming companion, designed to empower farmers with instant access to agricultural knowledge, personalized advice, and emotional support. Available in both English and Kannada, we bridge the gap between traditional farming wisdom and modern AI technology.

Our Purpose

To provide farmers with accessible, reliable, and timely agricultural information that helps improve crop yields, reduce losses, and make informed farming decisions.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted AI companion for farmers across India, empowering them with knowledge, technology, and support to build sustainable and prosperous agricultural communities.

Our Motive

To support farmers not just with agricultural advice, but also with emotional well-being and mental health support, recognizing the challenges and pressures of farming life.
